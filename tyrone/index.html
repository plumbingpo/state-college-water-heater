Getting More Years From Your Water Heater in Tyrone

A little routine care makes all the difference between a water heater that lasts and one that floods the garage in year seven. Our advice for homeowners is a short list: a yearly flush, an anode inspection every few years, a yearly test of the T&P valve, a thermostat set near 120 degrees, and annual descaling for tankless units on hard water, so problems are found while they're still inexpensive.

Age matters too. Most tank water heaters last 8 to 12 years, tankless units often reach 20 with regular descaling, and heat pump models usually run 10 to 15. If your heater is past that window and showing warning signs like rusty hot water, rumbling during heating, dampness under the tank, or hot water that runs out sooner than it used to, a planned replacement is almost always cheaper and much easier on you than an emergency one with water on the floor.

Minor issues don't stay minor for long. Consider a spent anode rod: replacing it is a quick, affordable repair, but if nobody replaces it, the water starts eating the tank steel, and just like that a minor fix becomes a full replacement. That's why we encourage homeowners to save our number in their phone and call at the first sign of trouble. Catching it early saves money and prevents a surprise cleanup.

Hot Water Headaches Tyrone Homeowners Face

In Tyrone, we work on every kind of water heater, and each one has a typical way of failing. Gas tanks usually give trouble through pilot assemblies and thermocouples, sooty burners, and venting that doesn't draft. Electric tanks lose heating elements and thermostats, particularly when sediment piles up around the lower element. Tankless units throw error codes for scale in the heat exchanger, ignition failures, and flow sensor trouble, most of which trace back to skipped descaling. Heat pump water heaters depend on a clean filter and a free-flowing condensate line, and they don't do well in small, closed-in spaces. We service all four types regularly and stock the common parts to finish most repairs in one trip.
